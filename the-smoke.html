WHAT YOU NEED

Foundry first.

  • Foundry Virtual Tabletop v14 according to the v0.5.15 manifest (minimum compatibility).
  • The host/GM needs a Foundry licence or hosted world; players do not need separate Smoke purchases to join that host.
  • Use your personal library entitlement and private download; never share the ZIP or installation link.
  • Forge-hosted installation and automatic updates are not yet independently verified.
  • Back up existing worlds before installing or changing game-system versions.
