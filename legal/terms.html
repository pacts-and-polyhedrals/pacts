The product

The Smoke is a digital tabletop roleplaying game system for Foundry VTT. It is not a standalone video game. One purchase grants the purchasing GM/host access for their installation; individual players joining that GM's hosted world are not required to purchase a separate licence. Copies or shareable manifest URLs must not be redistributed.

Founders Beta — $5

The first 100 successful purchases obtain Founders Beta access, future beta and release-candidate updates, and permanent Founder designation on their account. The $5 payment does not include the commercial finished edition. At release, Founders may buy a separate $15 upgrade (50% off $30), for a total of $20.

Expanded Early Access — $15

After the publisher manually activates this stage, new early-access buyers receive the current beta and pre-release updates. A future full-release upgrade costs an additional $15, for a total of $30.

Full Release — $30

At publisher-approved completion, new buyers pay $30. Existing beta customers receive personal account-specific $15 upgrade eligibility. Existing entitlements cannot be purchased a second time through the library.

Digital delivery

After Stripe verifies a successful payment server-side, delivery is made to My Library attached to the verified purchaser email. Private download and manifest capabilities may expire or be revoked when compromised. Customers recover access using a verified email link. Releases may be withdrawn for security, legal or compatibility reasons.

Compatibility

Foundry v14 is the package's manifest minimum; Forge support and automatic updates are not guaranteed until verified. A tested manual installation path will be supplied where appropriate. Always back up worlds. Current beta limitations are published separately.
