Refund requests

Customers should contact the publisher with their order receipt and purchase email. The final customer-support address and response time must be published before live sales.

Digital products

Whether cancellation rights apply after digital delivery varies by jurisdiction, the delivery/consent flow and mandatory local law. The checkout experience must collect any legally required acknowledgement separately. Do not rely on a blanket “all sales final” statement.

Refund and chargeback effects

Full refunds and chargebacks trigger an access review and revocation of compromised installation tokens. Users may request review when an entitlement was revoked in error. Refunds are issued through the original payment method using Stripe.

Before launch

Set concrete support details, jurisdiction-specific refunds language and digital-delivery consent in consultation with qualified counsel.
