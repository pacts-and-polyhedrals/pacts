Publisher policies

Privacy and customer data.

Draft for review · No public paid transactions authorised yet.

Privacy policy draft for owner approval and legal review. This template describes the intended system, not a claim that production data processing has begun.

Information needed for delivery

Customer email address, Stripe checkout/session and receipt identifiers, purchase date and price, access entitlements, optional marketing preference, and limited security/download records are processed to run the shop and prevent unauthorised access. Passwords and full card numbers are not collected by the website.

Service providers

Cloudflare Workers, D1 and private R2 store service and entitlement records; Stripe processes payments; Resend (if configured) sends login, receipt fulfilment and necessary update messages. Privacy terms from these processors also apply.

Marketing choice

Optional marketing emails require separate opt-in. Transactional access, security and fulfilment emails are distinct and may be sent independently as required to deliver a purchase.

Retention and rights

The final policy must specify controller contact, lawful bases, retention periods, cross-border transfers, data-request handling, cookies/session-storage use, and regional customer rights before payments are enabled.

Authentication and logs

One-use email links expire after 15 minutes. Browser session credentials are stored in this browser's session storage, not in a public URL. Private Foundry manifest URLs behave like passwords; keep them secret and revoke them if copied elsewhere.
