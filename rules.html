THE STREET
FIGHTS BACK.

Combat, movement, jobs and recovery all use the same shared fiction — and real consequences.

01 / DRIVING

MOTORS MATTER

Vehicle Performance replaces Effort on Wheels rolls; speed, handling, seats and chases matter. Take a taxi, or let the GM fast-travel any vehicle when a full drive isn't needed.

02 / CONSEQUENCES

HEAT IS REAL

Police notice witnessed crimes. Go loud, get clocked, then find somewhere you can lose them — and stay lost.

03 / GROWTH

REP TO 33

Grow through five tiers, gain stats, perks and Hustle, and build separate Renown with each faction.
